# Comali - Product Marketing Context

## 1. Product & Business Overview
- **Business**: B2B reseller of commercial cleaning equipment, dispensers, trash bins/containers, and concentrated cleaning chemicals.
- **Target Audience**: B2B decision makers (facility managers, purchasing officers, school admins, hospital procurement, restaurant/hotel managers, cleaning contractors).
- **Core Categories**:
  1. Lixeiras e Contentores (5L to 1000L, Inox, Coleta Seletiva, Pedal, Hospitalar, Contemar)
  2. Dispensers Banheiro (Saboneteiras, Toalheiros, Porta Rolão, Inox / Plástico, Biovis / Bralimpia)
  3. Produtos Químicos Concentrados (D&A linha CMI, Diluição, Frigorífico, Food Service, Hospitalar, Hotelaria)
- **Primary Conversion Channel**: Direct WhatsApp message (`{reference}: {title}`) to sales team. No direct e-commerce checkout.

## 2. Key Differentiators & Value Proposition
- High quality B2B durable products (inox, heavy-duty plastic, commercial grade).
- Direct WhatsApp quote & bulk purchase support.
- Specialized lines for regulated sectors (Hospitalar, Food Service, Coleta Seletiva).
- Complete solutions (dispenser + refill chemicals + trash management).

## 3. Customer Intent & Search Motives
- **High Intent Search**: Searching for specific commercial items (e.g. "lixeira inox pedal 50l b2b", "contentor lixo 1000l hospitalar", "dispenser saboneteira inox").
- **Commercial Brands Sold**: Contemar, Bralimpia, Biovis, D&A (CMI line). Note: Brand names removed from page titles/URLs for clean SEO, but products are genuine distribution. Do NOT negate these brand keywords!

## 4. Primary Campaign Goals & Constraints
- **Objective**: Lead generation via WhatsApp inquiries from qualified B2B buyers.
- **Budget Constraint**: R$ 90/day shared budget across Google Search campaigns.
- **Bidding Strategy Target**: Maximize Clicks with CPC teto (R$ 3.50 - R$ 4.00) during initial phase, transitioning to Target CPA / Max Conversions once GTM `whatsapp_click` conversion tracking matures (30+ conversions).
